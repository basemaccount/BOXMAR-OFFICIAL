Boxmar Food Tanks

Food-Grade Tank Operations

We are one of the premier food tank operators for your liquid food shipments. Boxmar provides specialized food-grade transport solutions ensuring product integrity, hygiene, and compliance with international food safety standards.

Our fleet includes Bulk Liner and Flexi Tank solutions designed specifically for the food and beverage industry, offering cost-effective alternatives to traditional drum or IBC transport.

  • Food-Grade Certified EquipmentAll tanks and liners meet international food safety certification requirements — ISO 22000 and HACCP compliant.
  • Precise Temperature ControlAccurate temperature management for sensitive products like edible oils, fruit concentrates, and dairy.
  • Single-Use Flexi TanksFood-grade bladders for contamination-free liquid transport up to 24,000L — cost-effective vs drums or IBCs.
  • Full Traceability & DocumentationComplete food safety documentation, HACCP-certified handling, and full chain-of-custody traceability.
