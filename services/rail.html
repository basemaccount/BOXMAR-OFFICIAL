International Rail Combined Transport

Boxmar strives to be the premier service supplier in international rail combined transport. Our rail freight services offer a cost-effective and environmentally friendly alternative for long-distance cargo movement.

Connecting major trade corridors between Turkey, Central Asia, and Europe, our rail solutions combine reliability with sustainability — ideal for bulk cargo and container shipments.

Key Advantages

Eco-Friendly

Up to 70% lower carbon footprint compared to road transport — the sustainable choice.

Cost-Effective

Competitive rates for long-distance shipments — significantly cheaper than air freight.

High Capacity

Large volume handling for bulk cargo, containers, and oversized shipments.

Multimodal

Seamless integration with road and sea transport for complete door-to-door solutions.
