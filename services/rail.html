International Rail Combined Transport

Boxmar strives to be the premier service supplier in international rail combined transport. Our rail freight services offer a cost-effective and environmentally friendly alternative for long-distance cargo movement.

Connecting major trade corridors between Turkey, Central Asia, and Europe, our rail solutions combine reliability with sustainability — ideal for bulk cargo and container shipments.

  • Eco-Friendly TransportUp to 70% lower carbon footprint compared to road transport — the sustainable choice for responsible businesses.
  • Cost-Effective Long DistanceCompetitive rates for long-distance shipments — up to 40% cheaper than air freight for comparable transit times.
  • High Volume CapacityLarge volume handling for bulk cargo, containers, and oversized shipments — ideal for heavy industry.
  • Seamless MultimodalIntegrated with road and sea transport for complete door-to-door solutions across any distance.
