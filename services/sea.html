Container Options

FCL vs LCL — Which Is Right for You?

We offer both options to match your cargo volume and budget requirements.

FCL — Full Container Load
Best for large shipments that fill an entire container
  • Exclusive use of 20ft or 40ft container
  • Lower cost per unit for large volumes
  • Faster transit — no consolidation delays
  • Reduced handling = less damage risk
  • Ideal for 10+ CBM shipments
  • Direct port-to-port or door-to-door
LCL — Less than Container Load
Best for smaller shipments sharing container space
  • Pay only for the space you use
  • Cost-effective for small volumes
  • Regular weekly consolidation services
  • Flexible for varying shipment sizes
  • Ideal for 1–10 CBM shipments
  • Worldwide consolidation network
