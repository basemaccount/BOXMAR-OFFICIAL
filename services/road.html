Complete Road Transport Solutions

Boxmar offers a complete service of fully integrated transportation all over Turkey and Europe. With a wide distribution and daily scheduled cargo routes with fixed timetables, we provide a fast and totally reliable service from the moment the collection order is received until the cargo is delivered to the consignee.
