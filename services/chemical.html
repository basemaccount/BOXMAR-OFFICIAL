Comprehensive Chemical Transport Solutions

We can provide a comprehensive service geared to your specific needs for your chemical and tank container shipments. As one of the leading tank operators in Turkey, Boxmar delivers exceptional service with specialized equipment and ADR-certified handling procedures.

Our chemical freight services cover the full range of liquid and gas transport from hazardous chemicals to industrial solvents, with stringent safety protocols and real-time temperature monitoring.

  • ADR / IMDG / RID CertifiedFull compliance with all international hazardous goods regulations — road, sea, and rail transport.
  • Precise Temperature ControlSteam and electric heating options. Range: -20°C to +130°C for temperature-sensitive chemical products.
  • Certified Tank CleaningCertified cleaning procedures between loads ensuring product purity and cross-contamination prevention.
  • 24/7 Real-Time MonitoringLive GPS tracking with continuous monitoring of location, temperature, and pressure throughout transit.

Our Process

Chemical Shipment Process

Every chemical shipment follows our rigorous 4-step safety protocol.

1
Booking & MSDS
Submit cargo details and Material Safety Data Sheet. We assess requirements and assign the right tank.
2
Tank Preparation
Certified tank cleaning, inspection, and preparation. Temperature pre-conditioning if required.
3
Loading & Sealing
ADR-certified loading with full documentation, sealing, and GPS tracker activation.
4
Delivery & POD
Safe delivery with proof of delivery, temperature logs, and full compliance documentation.
