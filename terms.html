Customer Obligations

The customer agrees to:

Provide accurate and complete information about the cargo, including weight, dimensions, value, and nature of goods
Declare all hazardous materials in accordance with applicable regulations (ADR, IMDG, IATA)
Provide all necessary documentation for customs clearance
Ensure cargo is properly packed and labeled
Pay all invoices within the agreed payment terms
Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
05

Liability & Limitations

Boxmar's liability as a freight forwarder is limited as follows:

Boxmar is not liable for loss or damage caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control (force majeure)
Maximum liability is limited to the lower of: (a) the actual value of the goods, or (b) 2 SDR per kilogram of gross weight
Boxmar is not liable for consequential, indirect, or special damages
Claims must be submitted in writing within 14 days of delivery (or expected delivery date)
These limitations do not apply in cases of willful misconduct or gross negligence by Boxmar.

Cargo Insurance

Boxmar strongly recommends that customers obtain adequate cargo insurance. Boxmar can arrange cargo insurance upon request at the customer's expense.

Standard freight forwarder liability does not constitute full cargo insurance coverage. We recommend insuring all shipments.

Dangerous Goods

The transport of dangerous goods is subject to strict regulations. Customers must:

Declare all dangerous goods in advance
Provide proper documentation (MSDS, dangerous goods declaration)
Ensure proper packaging and labeling per ADR / IMDG / IATA regulations
Obtain all necessary permits and approvals
