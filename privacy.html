Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Boxmar Logistics Transportation Co. Ltd. ("Boxmar", "we", "us", or "our"), located at Muratreis Mah. Payanda Sk., Çelik İş Hanı No:9 İç Kapı No:1, Üsküdar, İstanbul, Turkey, is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

This policy complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK - Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu No. 6698).

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name
  • Shipment Data: Origin/destination, cargo type, weight, special requirements
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our contact form or chat widget
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on site
  • Cookie Data: Session cookies and analytics cookies (see Section 7)

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract Performance: To provide freight forwarding and logistics services you have requested
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our services, respond to inquiries, and maintain business records
  • Consent: For marketing communications (where you have opted in)
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Process and manage freight forwarding quotations and shipments
  • Communicate with you about your shipments and inquiries
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send service-related notifications and updates
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

5. Data Sharing and Transfers

We may share your personal data with:

  • Carriers and Partners: Shipping lines, airlines, trucking companies, and customs brokers necessary to fulfill your shipment
  • Service Providers: IT service providers, email service providers (EmailJS), and analytics providers
  • Regulatory Authorities: Customs authorities, port authorities, and other government bodies as required by law
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. International transfers are conducted with appropriate safeguards in place.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Typically:

  • Shipment records: 10 years (Turkish Commercial Code requirement)
  • Customer inquiry data: 3 years
  • Website analytics: 2 years
  • Marketing consent records: Until consent is withdrawn

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. We use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how visitors use our website (Google Analytics)
  • Functional Cookies: To remember your preferences (language selection)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@boxmar.com.tr. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include SSL/TLS encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
